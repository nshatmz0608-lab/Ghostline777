The Workshop

Four layers of customization

Every upgrade changes how the car drives, not just how it looks. Tuning is saved per car and can be shared with your crew.

01

Engine & Drivetrain

Turbo swaps, ECU maps, gear ratios and diff locking, with live dyno readouts as you tune.

02

Suspension & Aero

Ride height, camber, damping and downforce. Set the car up for drift, grip or drag.

03

Body & Livery

Widebody kits, 600+ vinyl layers, matte, chrome and chameleon paints with custom decals.

04

Street Identity

Underglow, tire-smoke color, exhaust note and plate text. Make sure rivals know it's you.
