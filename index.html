The Story

When the city sleeps, Los Santos races.

Under the freeways and behind the shuttered docks, a crew called the Ghostline runs the most dangerous circuit on the West Coast.

You arrive in Los Santos with a borrowed coupe and a debt to the wrong people. To pay it off you'll race through the streets of Vinewood, the Port of South Los Santos and the Del Perro coastline after midnight, building a reputation one duel at a time.

Every win pulls you deeper into the crew's inner circle. Every loss costs you parts, cash and respect. The LSPD Night Division is tracking the crew, and the closer you get to the top, the harder they push.

Game Highlights

Built for the night shift

Three systems sit at the core of Ghostline. Each one comes from real driving physics, real tuning culture and real street racing tension.

01

Real-Time Physics

Each tire is simulated on its own, with live weight transfer, so wet asphalt, curb strikes and a bad lift-off all feel different through the wheel.

  • Simulated at 360 Hz
  • Dynamic wet-road grip
  • Damage you can see on the body
02

Full Customization

Change everything from the turbo housing to the rear diffuser. Build a drift car, a drag car or something in between, and paint it however you like.

  • 1,400+ performance parts
  • Layered livery editor
  • Underglow, smoke and horn tuning
03

Night Street Duels

Flash your lights at any racer cruising the city to start a one-on-one duel. Put your car's pink slip on the line, or a week's worth of rep.

  • Live 1v1 & 8-player crew wars
  • Pink-slip wagers
  • Police pursuits mid-race
